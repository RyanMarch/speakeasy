Menu Builder & Event Menus

Transform your recipe collection into elegant cocktail menus for dinner parties, seasonal gatherings, and home tastings.

When hosting friends or family, presenting a focused list of featured drinks simplifies ordering and makes entertaining feel intentional. Speakeasy lets you compose, customize, and print menus in minutes.

Creating an Event Menu

Starting a new cocktail menu is simple:

  1. Navigate to the Menu Builder view from the application drawer or via Saved Event Menus in Settings.
  2. Select Create Menu and assign a festive title (such as Autumn Tasting Night or Rooftop Soirée).
  3. Add an optional welcome subtitle or greeting to introduce your drink selection to guests.

Selecting Cocktails & Courses

Curate a balanced lineup suited to your evening:

  • Browse Your Catalog: Pick from classic staples or your own signature custom riffs.
  • Check Backbar Feasibility: Speakeasy highlights which drinks on your proposed menu are fully supported by your current stock so you never run dry mid-event.
  • Group by Sections: Organize drinks into courses such as Welcome Aperitifs, Main Features, and After-Dinner Nightcaps.

Presentation & Printing

Once your selections are set, bring the menu to life:

  • Digital Bar Card: Display your active event menu on a counter tablet where guests can tap individual items to read tasting notes and lore.
  • Print-Ready Export: Format your menu for crisp cardstock printing with tailored typography, deco borders, and drink descriptions.
