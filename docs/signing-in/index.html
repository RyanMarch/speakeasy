Signing In & Cloud Sync

Keep your cocktail library, custom recipes, and home inventory seamlessly synced across your devices with password-free security.

Speakeasy runs completely offline and in private guest mode out of the box. When you are ready to access your recipes anywhere, signing in creates an automatic cloud backup without cumbersome passwords.

Passwordless Email Sign-In

Instead of making you memorize another password that can get compromised, Speakeasy relies on secure one-time verification codes sent to your inbox:

  1. Tap Sign In in the top header, or select Sign In to Sync inside Vault Settings.
  2. Enter your email address and submit.
  3. Check your email for a crisp 6-digit verification code.
  4. Enter the code in the verification modal. Once verified, your session remains securely authenticated for 30 days.

Effortless Guest Migration

If you built a bottle list or customized cocktails before creating an account, none of that effort is lost:

  • First Sign-In Merge: Speakeasy identifies all local bottles, custom riffs, event menus, and pour records on your device and uploads them directly to your new cloud vault.
  • No Overwrites: Existing cloud data blends together intelligently so you can pick up right where you left off on either phone or desktop.

Cloud Sync Status & Multi-Device Access

Once authenticated, the header profile pill displays your current sync health:

  • Green Dot: All recent edits, stock changes, and notes are fully saved to the secure cloud.
  • Yellow Dot: Local updates are queued and will automatically sync as soon as network connectivity is restored.
  • Manual Sync: Open Settings anytime and tap Sync Now to immediately fetch recent changes made on your other devices.

Signing Out & Account Control

You always retain absolute control over your personal data:

  • Signing Out: Clearing your session removes cached personal data from the current browser while preserving everything safely in your cloud vault.
  • Account Deletion: If you ever choose to delete your account, you can permanently purge your cloud profile and synchronized data under the Settings Danger Zone.
