Responsible Consumption Disclaimer

Legal Age Requirement: Speakeasy is intended solely for individuals of legal drinking age in their respective jurisdiction (21 years of age or older in the United States). By accessing this site or using the application, you confirm that you meet this requirement.

Speakeasy provides beverage recipes, ingredient ratios, historical notes, and estimated alcohol by volume (ABV) for informational and entertainment purposes only. Calculations for dilution, ABV, and proof are mathematical estimates and should never be used as a measure of physical impairment, blood alcohol concentration, or fitness to operate motor vehicles or heavy machinery.

Always enjoy responsibly and never drink and drive. If you or someone you know is struggling with alcohol misuse, assistance is available through resources such as SAMHSA’s National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357.

Privacy Policy

Speakeasy was architected on a foundation of privacy-by-design and data minimization. We believe the tools you use in your home or behind the bar should respect your privacy.

1. Local-First Storage (Guest Mode)

When you use Speakeasy without signing in, 100% of your data (favorite recipes, personal inventory, custom cocktails, notes, and menus) remains strictly in your browser's local storage (localStorage). This data is never sent to our servers.

2. Optional Cloud Synchronization

If you choose to sync your collection across devices, you may register using a passwordless email verification code (OTP). We store only:

  • Your email address (solely for authenticating your sign-in sessions)
  • Your synchronized bar inventories and custom recipes
  • Your drink pouring history log (if enabled)

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your email or personal cocktail library with advertisers or third parties.

3. Privacy-Preserving Telemetry

To understand which cocktail recipes are popular and resolve broken search terms, Speakeasy collects minimal, non-identifying telemetry events (e.g. recipe page views, coarse device class like mobile or desktop, and anonymous search queries).

  • We do not track or log IP addresses in our telemetry database.
  • We do not load third-party ad pixels, social trackers, or behavioral analytics cookies.
  • Telemetry events are never linked to your personal email or account ID.

Data Rights, Export & Account Deletion

You have complete authority over your bar data:

  • Full JSON Export: You can download a complete backup of all custom recipes, backbar bottles, and saved menus directly in your browser anytime from the Settings page.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: If you created a cloud account, you can permanently delete your entire profile, bar inventories, and synchronized records immediately from the Settings page. This action permanently purges your records from the database and cannot be undone.
